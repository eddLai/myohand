SM2 status byte, sampled every frame. bufState 3 means "nothing written since the last read";
0-2 name which of the three buffers was written last. rdInUse is the PDI - the hand's
own controller - holding a buffer open to read it.

How to read it: if the rows that fail differ from the rows that
work in the bufState/rdInUse columns, the sync manager is where
it breaks and the application never completing a read is the
mechanism. If those columns look the same either way, the fault
is above the SM and meas_us versus period_us is the next clue.
